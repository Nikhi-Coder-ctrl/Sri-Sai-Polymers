About Sri Sai Polymers

Practical recycling solutions built around industrial needs.

Sri Sai Polymers is a plastic recycling business focused on recovering and processing materials such as HDPE and LDPE for reuse in industrial applications.

We work with suppliers, transport partners, recyclers, manufacturers, and buyers to move material efficiently through the recycling chain while maintaining a strong focus on quality, reliability, and responsible operations.

Recycling Process

Turning discarded plastic into usable material.

Our process is designed around material recovery, preparation, reprocessing, and delivery.

01

Collection & Sourcing

Plastic scrap is sourced from suitable suppliers and industrial channels.

02

Sorting

Material is segregated based on polymer type, condition, colour, and contamination level.

03

Cleaning & Preparation

Selected plastics are prepared for processing by removing unwanted material and contamination.

04

Reprocessing

Material is mechanically processed into a reusable form suitable for subsequent manufacturing.

05

Quality Check

Processed material is checked for consistency before packing and dispatch.

06

Supply & Dispatch

Finished recycled material is packed and coordinated for delivery to customers.

Sustainability

Keeping plastics in use for longer.

Recycling helps reduce landfill pressure, recover value from discarded material, and decrease the need for virgin polymer in suitable applications.

Our goal is simple: create useful material from plastic waste while improving resource efficiency across the supply chain.
